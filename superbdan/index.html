Project Overview

A technical SEO audit of superbdan.com, a WordPress tech news and reviews site, completed in November 2026. It evaluates crawlability, indexability, site architecture and on-page metadata, and ends with a phased remediation roadmap.

What Went Well

The foundation is strong: zero 5xx errors or timeouts, HTTPS enforced, host and trailing-slash canonicalization intact, no soft 404s, and no orphan pages.

What Needs Work

Template-level issues waste crawl budget and dilute link equity: crawlable internal search URLs (1,415 links to 119 variations), 3,516 internal links hitting legacy http:// redirects, malformed author pagination that creates 404 loops, and indexable paginated archives.

Scope Note

Google Search Console access was not available, so GSC sitemap submission, parameter settings and low-traffic page analysis are marked as not audited.

Key Findings

Internal Search (/?s=) Crawlable

High impact · Low effort

1,415 internal links point to 119 search-parameter URLs, creating infinite low-value URL variations. Block via robots.txt and add noindex, follow.

Paginated Archives Indexable

High impact · Medium effort

39 paginated archive URLs (/page/2/ to /page/13/) are open to indexation. Set page 2+ to noindex, follow.

Canonicals to Parameter URLs

High impact · Low effort

5 author filter URLs (?section=review) fragment link equity. Point links and canonicals to the clean master URLs.

Malformed Author Pagination

Medium impact · Medium effort

Archive logic appends author names (e.g. /page/12/Dantebisais), producing 404s and pushing content past 4 clicks deep.

Legacy HTTP Redirect Hops

Medium impact · Low effort

3,516 internal links in the footer and off-canvas menu use http:// and trigger a 301 on every page view.

Internal & Social 4xx Links

Medium impact · Low effort

15 internal URLs (23 inlinks) return 4xx, mostly relative X/Twitter handles in author bio boxes.

Outdated XML Sitemap

Medium impact · Low effort

sitemap.xml lists redirected and non-canonical URLs. Regenerate with 200-OK canonical URLs only.

Accidental noindex

Low impact · Low effort

A published review article carries a noindex meta tag. Switch it to index, follow.
