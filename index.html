Get to Know Me

Building things with SEO in mind

I did link prospecting for five years. The work was straightforward, but I was curious about the numbers. I'd see metrics on worksheets and think, there's more to this. What's the bigger picture? What's behind these numbers?

So I dug into Technical SEO and got sucked into it. I already had a background in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from college, so I took a full-stack bootcamp as a refresher to actually see how everything works at the code level. Not because I wanted to be a developer, but because I needed to understand the full context.

Now I can identify problems and implement solutions. I think carefully before moving. I troubleshoot instead of guessing. I look for ways to improve how things work. I'm easily teachable and adapt to how you work and new technologies and updates, but I'll ask questions first to make sure I understand.

How I work: I prefer written or chat communication over calls, but I can adjust when needed. I work independently after understanding what matters and how you position things. My process is iterative, whether I'm solving something urgent or thinking through how to streamline a process. I research thoroughly because good decisions come from understanding your options. I prioritize high-impact work over busywork.

I'm solid at a few things and keep learning the rest. I don't have real client work yet, but I've built solid local projects that demonstrate what I can do. I bring that same curiosity I had five years ago, but now I actually know where to look for answers.
